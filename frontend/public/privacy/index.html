Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 5, 2026 | Last Updated: October 5, 2026

Applicord ("we," "our," or "us") provides a job application tracking and career management workspace accessible at https://applicord.vercel.app. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you access our website, applications, and related services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information necessary to provide our application tracking features:

  • Account Details: When you register, we collect your name, email address, password hash (salted using bcrypt), and preferred timezone.
  • Job Application Records: Information you manually enter or import, including company names, job titles, application links, notes, status history, interview dates, and contacts.
  • User-Uploaded Documents: Resumes, cover letters, and application notes you voluntarily store in your workspace.

2. Google User Data (Gmail Integration)

Applicord offers an optional Google OAuth integration that allows users to automatically track job application progress from their email. When you link your Google account, Applicord requests the following permission:

  • Scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly (View your email messages and settings in read-only mode).

Specific Data Accessed:

  • Email message metadata (Sender address, Subject line, Date received, Message ID).
  • Message body snippet and text content relevant to detecting job application submissions, interview requests, status changes, and assessment invitations.

Purpose of Use: We access this data strictly to identify job application updates and suggest status changes to your Applicord dashboard. We do not compose, send, alter, or delete messages in your inbox.

Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance:
Applicord's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

3. Strict Limits on Google User Data

In accordance with Google Limited Use requirements, we enforce the following restrictions:

  • No Advertising: We never use or transfer Google user data for serving advertisements, retargeting, or personalized marketing.
  • No Sale or Transfer: We do not sell, rent, or lease Google user data or any personal information to data brokers, advertising networks, or third parties.
  • No AI Model Training: Google user data is never used to train, retrain, or improve generalized artificial intelligence (AI) or machine learning (ML) models.
  • No Human Access: No human employee, contractor, or agent reads your email messages unless:
    • You provide explicit written consent for troubleshooting a specific technical issue;
    • It is necessary for security purposes (such as investigating security abuse or system errors); or
    • It is required to comply with applicable law or legal processes.

4. Data Storage and Security

We implement robust technical and organizational measures to secure your personal data:

  • OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens are encrypted at rest using authenticated AES-256-GCM encryption.
  • All data in transit is encrypted using modern TLS (HTTPS) protocols.
  • Database connections utilize pooled, encrypted connections hosted in protected cloud environments.

5. Data Retention, Disconnection, and Deletion

You maintain complete control over your data at all times:

  • Disconnecting Gmail: You can disconnect your connected Gmail account at any time from your Applicord Settings. Disconnecting immediately removes your stored OAuth tokens from our database.
  • Revoking Permissions: You can also revoke Applicord's access to your Google Account at any time via Google Account Security Permissions.
  • Complete Account Deletion: You may request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated records by contacting us at ssworkstech@gmail.com. Upon request, all data is permanently purged within 30 days.
